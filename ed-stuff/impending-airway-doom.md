## generic approach
- Escalate and ask for help early
- Where is the place of safety? → resus, theatres?
- Sit patient up, keep calm

#### `airway doom from infections, abscesses etc.`
- Escalate and ask for help early
- Where is the place of safety? → resus, theatres?
- Sit patient up, keep calm
- IV Dexamethasone? Adrenaline neb?
